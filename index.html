Process

You bring the product. I make the story easier to see.

The process stays collaborative, but it does not need to become complicated. The goal is to remove uncertainty early so production stays focused.

01 · DISCOVERY

Understand

Start with the real problem.

I learn the product, audience, objective and action you want the viewer to take. The point is to understand what matters before touching the timeline.

02 · STRUCTURE

Shape

Decide what earns screen time.

I organize the message, script, storyboard, product screens and visual direction so the video has a clear path instead of a list of features.

03 · MOTION

Create

Build the piece frame by frame.

I handle editing, motion graphics, UI animation, screen recordings, typography, transitions and visual storytelling.

04 · DELIVERY

Refine

No surprises at the finish line.

You review, I refine, and the final assets are prepared for your website, launch, campaign, social channels or tutorial library.

Frequently asked questions

A few things clients usually want to know before we start.

Still deciding?

The fastest route is a short call, but these answers cover the practical questions that usually come first.

What kinds of videos do you create?

SaaS demos, product marketing videos, feature promos, explainers, UI animation, tutorials, onboarding content and other motion-led product communication.

Can you work from an existing script or storyboard?

Yes. I can work from a finished script/storyboard, or help organize the message and visual direction when you only have product notes, a website, Figma screens or a rough brief.

Can you animate my actual product UI?

Yes. Product screens, screen recordings and UI-focused motion are a major part of my work. The exact workflow depends on what source material you can provide.

How long does a project take?

It depends on length, complexity, available assets and review cycles. A short product piece can move quickly; longer explainers and tutorial systems need more time. I confirm the production plan after reviewing the brief.

How do revisions work?

I build review points into the process so feedback arrives before the project is too far down the wrong path. The exact number and scope of revisions are agreed for each project before production begins.

Do you also create tutorials and long-form product education?

Yes. I have a separate body of work covering walkthroughs, training videos, onboarding and product education in addition to short marketing pieces.

Can I hire you through Upwork or Fiverr?

Yes. If your team prefers platform-based contracts, you can hire me through either Upwork or Fiverr. Direct project discussions can also start by email or Calendly.

What do you need from me to get started?

A product link or demo, the goal of the video, the intended audience, any existing script or messaging, brand assets, examples you like, and your target timeline. If some of that is missing, we can work through it together.
